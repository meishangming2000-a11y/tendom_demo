## Metric Definitions
- `curl range`: max minus min of input global open/close curl over the video-derived sequence.
- `tip dist delta`: mean fingertip-to-palm distance at the most-open frame minus the same distance at the most-closed frame; positive means fingertips moved closer to the palm.
- `tip spread reduction`: mean pairwise fingertip spacing at the most-open frame minus the same spacing at the most-closed frame; positive means the hand closed inward.
- `thumb-index close`: thumb tip to index tip distance at the most-closed frame.
- `joint activity`: normalized closed-minus-open motion over the main flexion joints, divided by each joint range.
- `closure score`: diagnostic scalar in [0, 1] combining tip-palm reduction, tip-spread reduction, thumb-index reduction, and joint activity. It is useful for comparing trends across videos, not as a universal grasp-success score.
